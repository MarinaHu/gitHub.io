Reflection

From Research to Communication

Publishing scientific research taught me that generating evidence is only part of the process. Scientific findings also need to be interpreted, organized, challenged, revised, and communicated clearly enough for others to understand and use.

Across my research projects, I developed experience synthesizing literature, interpreting quantitative findings, building scientific narratives, creating figures and presentations, responding to peer-review feedback, and collaborating with researchers across disciplines.

These experiences now form an important foundation for my work in medical and scientific communications.
